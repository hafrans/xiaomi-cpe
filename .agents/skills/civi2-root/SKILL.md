# Civi 2 Root

A past successful Root is evidence for one boot, not the current state. Before device operations, confirm the connected device, build, bootloader and Root status. Preserve the locked bootloader and existing data; do not flash or reset as an inferred step.

Read only the relevant record:

- Verified outcome and open questions: [spec.md](../../../specs/001-civi2-root/spec.md).
- Tools, hashes, actual attempts and recovery boundaries: [root log](../../../docs/civi2-root-log.md).
- Scope, evidence mapping and remaining verification: [plan.md](../../../specs/001-civi2-root/plan.md) and [tasks.md](../../../specs/001-civi2-root/tasks.md).
- CPE and kernel feasibility: [research](../../../docs/cpe-feasibility.md).

After an authorized device change, append observed results and distinguish them from assumptions; never mark reboot persistence or OpenWrt compatibility as verified without a test.
